About Chloe G's

Chloe G's Homemade Treats started as a weekend baking hobby and grew into a little popup stand. Everything is made by hand in small batches, from sourdough scones and cinnamon rolls to brown-butter cookies and rice crispy treats.

Allergens

Our treats are made in a home kitchen and commonly contain wheat, dairy, and eggs; some contain tree nuts. Each item lists its ingredients and allergens on its page. If you have a severe allergy, please reach out before ordering.
